Yasser Mejía
Head of User Experience
🇳🇮 Nicaragua · based in 🇨🇳 China

Yasser is originally from Nicaragua and is currently studying at University of International Business and Economics (UIBE). With experience in both Taiwan and mainland China, he was part of the first group of Nicaraguan students selected for the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) to study in mainland China. He brings experience in partnership building, stakeholder engagement, and cross-cultural communication from his internship at the Embassy of the Republic of Nicaragua in China, where he supported trade and investment promotion and became the first Nicaraguan student selected as an official delegate at the China International Import Expo (CIIE) 2025 in Shanghai — coordinating with government officials, exhibitors, and investors on behalf of his country. He works trilingually across Spanish, English, and Chinese, connecting Asia to global markets. Outside of his work, Yasser is passionate about photography, often capturing the people and places he encounters while navigating between cultures — a lens that also shapes how he approaches storytelling in his media and partnerships work.
