How the Academy works

A tutor, a group, a real workshop

👩‍🏫

Expert tutor

Every workshop is led by a qualified, native-level tutor who guides the group from the front.

👥

Small groups of learners

Learners join a cohort, so everyone gets real attention and plenty of speaking time.

📍

In-person workshop

Sessions run face-to-face at a partner venue — structured, hands-on and social.

💳

Paid enrollment

Learners pay to join a course of workshops, so tutors are fairly paid and quality stays high.
