How a session works

A 1–2 hour online panel

1

Welcome & Intro

A brief introduction of the speakers, participants, and the Collective.

2

Speaker's Talk

Invited speakers share their experience and perspective on language & culture content creation.

3

Insights Exchange

A moderated discussion with pre-prepared questions, so speakers trade practical, real-world insights.

4

Audience Q&A

Participants ask questions and engage directly with the speakers.
